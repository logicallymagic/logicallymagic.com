## What ties them together

- **ML applied to a concrete P&L line.** Best execution, liquidity
  prediction, valuation accuracy — each model answers a question a business
  is already paying to answer badly.
- **Data access as a first-class problem.** Open banking, healthcare, and
  identity products prove that secure, standards-compliant data access is
  itself an AI enabler.
- **Non-intrusive by design.** From FAPI-compliant consent to three golf
  sensors — the pattern is the same: intelligence without disrupting the
  existing workflow.
