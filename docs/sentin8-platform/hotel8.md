# Hotel8

<div className="doc-image-frame">
  <img src="/img/project-grid/hotel8.jpg" alt="Hotel8" />
</div>

Hotel8 is the hospitality vertical of the Sentin8 platform: property
management, point of sale, channel distribution, metasearch, loyalty, and
guest upsell as one composable vertical.

## What it is

Everything a hotel or hotel group runs on — the PMS, the POS, the channel
manager, loyalty, and upsell — composed as one vertical instead of a
patchwork of vendors with nightly CSV reconciliations between them.

## The business case

- **One system of record for the property.** Reservations, folios, POS
  charges, and loyalty all land in the same event-sourced core — no more
  night-audit reconciliation between disconnected vendors.
- **Sell on every channel without overbooking.** Channel distribution and
  metasearch integration push live availability to OTAs and comparison
  sites from a single inventory pool.
- **Loyalty that actually pays off.** Native loyalty accrual and redemption
  across both room revenue and F&B (POS) spend, which is where most
  loyalty programs leak value.
- **Upsell as a product, not a project.** Guest upsell capabilities are
  built in — late checkout, room upgrades, early check-in — driving RevPAR
  without third-party tools.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Property management** | Reservations, housekeeping, rate plans, and folios. |
| **Point of sale** | F&B and retail charges posted straight to the room or ledger. |
| **Channel distribution** | Live inventory across OTAs with a single availability pool. |
| **Metasearch** | Presence on the comparison sites where travel decisions start. |
| **Loyalty** | Earning and burning across the full guest spend, not just rooms. |
| **Guest upsell** | Built-in upsell flows that lift RevPAR per available room. |

## Who it's for

- **Independent hotels and groups** replacing a fragile PMS + POS +
  channel-manager patchwork.
- **Boutique chains** that want loyalty and upsell without enterprise
  pricing.
- **Hospitality tech providers** building on a composable platform.
