# sorPRIME

<div className="doc-image-frame">
  <img src="/img/project-grid/sor-thumb.jpg" alt="sorPRIME" />
</div>

sorPRIME (SOR′) is a smart order router that finds the best way to execute a
trade using predefined business rules and AI (machine learning) to enhance
the investors' ability to access liquidity in the market and receive the
best prices and execution. It is a flexible, highly extensible, and
ultra-low latency smart order routing platform designed to support the
varied trading needs of execution venues, sell-side and buy-side firms.

## The business case

- **Best execution is a regulatory duty — and a differentiator.** MiFID II,
  SEC Rule 603, and CIRO obligations all require demonstrable best
  execution. sorPRIME doesn't just comply; it turns execution quality into
  a marketing asset you can show clients.
- **Dark pools are where alpha hides.** Machine learning models predict the
  distribution of liquidity in dark pools, so you can reach into venues
  others avoid — without paying the price of fishing blindly.
- **Per-instrument optimization compounds.** ML optimizes the numerical
  details per stock: queue position, spread capture, and venue-specific
  behaviour. Small per-trade improvements compound into measurable annual
  basis-point gains.
- **Risk management in the loop.** Routing decisions respect risk limits
  before they hit the wire, so an aggressive optimization never becomes a
  compliance incident.

## What sorPRIME focuses on

- **Best execution** — use of machine learning to optimize the numerical
  details per stock.
- **Handling of dark pools** — use of machine learning to predict the
  distribution of liquidity in the pool.
- **Risk management** — limits enforced in the routing hot path.

## Who it's for

- **Execution venues** that must demonstrate best execution to participants
  and regulators.
- **Sell-side firms** whose routing decisions directly affect client
  outcomes and commissions.
- **Buy-side firms** seeking measurable execution improvement without
  building an internal quant team.
