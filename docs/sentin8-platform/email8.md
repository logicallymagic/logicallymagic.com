# Email8

  <img src="/img/project-grid/email8.jpg" alt="Email8" />
</div>

Email8 is the email and collaboration vertical of the Sentin8 platform: an
SMTP/LMTP transfer engine, a mail and collaboration data core with JMAP, and
full-protocol mailbox access.

## What it is

A from-scratch email infrastructure stack — the transfer engine that moves
mail, the data core that stores it, and the protocol layer that clients
talk to. Not a wrapper around legacy open-source components.

## The business case

- **Own your communication layer.** For regulated businesses, email is
  business-critical data — hosting it on infrastructure you control (with
  Sentin8's audit trail) is a compliance and sovereignty advantage.
- **JMAP for the modern era.** JMAP means efficient mobile clients, instant
  sync, and no IMAP-idle battery drain — a materially better user
  experience.
- **Full-protocol mailbox access.** Your existing users and clients keep
  working during migration; nothing is forced to switch on day one.
- **Collaboration beyond mail.** The data core treats mail, calendars, and
  contacts as one collaboration dataset instead of three silos.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **SMTP/LMTP transfer engine** | High-throughput, policy-aware mail transfer. |
| **Mail & collaboration data core** | One dataset for mail, calendars, and contacts. |
| **JMAP** | Modern, efficient client sync for web and mobile. |
| **Full-protocol mailbox access** | Compatibility with existing clients and tools. |
| **Sentin8 tenancy** | Per-tenant isolation for every mailbox, enforced by the platform. |

## Who it's for

- **Enterprises and regulated firms** that need sovereign, auditable email
  infrastructure.
- **Platform companies** offering communication products to their customers.
- **MSPs and telcos** modernizing legacy mail stacks.
