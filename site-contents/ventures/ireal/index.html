• iReal.Ai

    Interactive & Fast Predictive Real Estate AI Platform

iReal.Ai is an Interactive & Fast Predictive Real Estate A.I. platform providing solutions currently not available on any other real estate platform. A.I. mines and converts all related documents and information to searchable filters and criteria while using AR, NLP and screen to visualize both query and data, thus:

  • Reducing real estate professionals' hours of workload to a few seconds.
  • Allowing the general public to perform hyper-personalized searches.
  • Providing appraisal & valuation to both real estate professionals and the public.

Features:

  • A.I. Real-Time Appraisal: You can also fine-tune the outcome based on extra variables and intelligence you have on the property.
  • Over 200 Search Criteria: A.I. mines and converts all related documents and information to searchable filters and criteria.
  • Interactive A.I. Assistant: Replies to users’ requests in natural language, while using screen to visualize the query and data.
  • Super-fast Search: Answers the most complex queries in real-time - same queries with MLS® would take hours to conduct.
  • A.I. Rating: Compares and rates selected properties based on best-value-for-money.
  • Affordability: Financial-based search.
  • Augmented Reality: Point your mobile toward a building and get all available listings.
  • Scanner: Zoom your mobile on a MLS® number or address and the listing will appear.
