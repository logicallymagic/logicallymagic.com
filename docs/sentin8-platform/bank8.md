# Bank8

  <img src="/img/project-grid/bank8.jpg" alt="Bank8" />
</div>

Bank8 is the banking, payments, and open banking vertical of the Sentin8
platform: a core ledger with double-entry postings, ISO 20022 payment
orchestration, and FDX v6.5 data exchange.

## What it is

A bank-grade core built on the same event-sourced, zero-static-secret
substrate as the rest of the platform. Money movements are immutable,
double-entry postings — never balance edits — so every cent is reconcilable
at any point in time.

## The business case

- **Launch neobank features in weeks, not years.** Prebuilt ledger, payment
  orchestration, and open-banking rails remove the classic core-banking
  procurement bottleneck.
- **ISO 20022 out of the box.** Orchestrate payments across correspondents
  and schemes using the message standard regulators are converging on.
- **Open banking as revenue.** FDX v6.5 data exchange means you can offer
  account aggregation and data products to fintechs — compliant from day one.
- **Reconciliation you can trust.** Double-entry postings make the ledger
  self-auditing; month-end close stops being a firefight.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Double-entry core ledger** | Every movement balances by construction; audit-proof money. |
| **ISO 20022 payment orchestration** | Scheme-agnostic payment processing with the global message standard. |
| **FDX v6.5 data exchange** | Open banking data sharing for fintech and enterprise partnerships. |
| **Sentin8 tenancy & identity** | Mandatory tenant isolation for every customer and account. |
| **Event-sourced history** | Full replay of any account's history for dispute resolution. |

## Who it's for

- **Neobanks and challenger banks** that need a compliant core without a
  decade of integration work.
- **Fintechs holding or moving customer money** that need a ledger which
  survives audits.
- **Enterprises** building embedded finance on top of their existing
  products.
