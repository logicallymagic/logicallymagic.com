# iEHR

<div className="doc-image-frame">
  <img src="/img/project-grid/iEHR.jpg" alt="iEHR" />
</div>

iEHR is a distributed healthcare platform that helps you deliver **any**
healthcare product or service — one patient record, distributed delivery,
and interoperability from day one.

## The business case

- **Healthcare delivery is distributed; most records aren't.** Clinics,
  labs, pharmacies, and specialists all touch one patient. iEHR's
  distributed architecture mirrors how care actually happens, instead of
  forcing every participant through one central bottleneck.
- **Any product, any service.** The platform is built so new healthcare
  offerings — telehealth, remote monitoring, specialized clinics — can be
  launched on the existing record, rather than each spawning its own silo.
- **Interoperability as a starting point.** A patient record that already
  speaks to the ecosystem means partnership deals move from legal review
  to production quickly.
- **A track record with real institutions.** iEHR is deployed with
  healthcare organizations (see our healthcare clients on the
  [About page](/about)) — this is production software, not a concept.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **One distributed record** | Every provider and service reads and writes the same patient history. |
| **Product & service delivery layer** | Launch new healthcare offerings on shared infrastructure. |
| **Interoperability** | Data flows between participants without bespoke integrations. |
| **Proven deployment** | Running in production with institutional partners. |
