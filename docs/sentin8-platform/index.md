**Sentin8** is the post-quantum, zero-static-secret platform for regulated
multi-vertical software. One identity plane, live authorization, and
mandatory tenant isolation — the foundation every logically MAGIC vertical
is built on.

## Why it matters to your business

- **Regulatory readiness as a feature.** Mandatory tenant isolation and a
  complete audit trail mean compliance evidence is generated as a side
  effect of normal operation — not assembled by hand before an audit.
- **No static secrets to rotate, leak, or forget.** Post-quantum key
  exchange removes an entire class of breach headlines.
- **One identity plane.** Onboard a customer once; every vertical they use
  inherits the same identity, roles, and policy — reducing operational cost
  and support load as you expand.
- **Live authorization.** Permission changes take effect immediately across
  every service, so revoking access is never a waiting game.

## The verticals

The platform ships as a set of composable verticals. Each one is a complete,
production-grade product that inherits Sentin8's security and tenancy model
on day one.
