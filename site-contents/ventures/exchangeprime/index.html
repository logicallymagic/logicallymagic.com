    An Advanced Hybrid Securities Exchange

exchangePRIME is a highly-optimized and ultra-low latency hybrid securities exchange platform. It can act as Exchange, ECN, MTF (Europe), or ATS (Canada). It is designed to be operated by any investment firm or market operator (brokers, market makers, investment banks, stock exchanges) that brings together multiple third-party buying and selling interests in financial instruments.

The platform is built on a rule-based, distributed, and fault tolerant technology, enabling it to be highly reliable, scalable, and flexible. It also provides sub-micrososecond connectivity for HFT and algorithmic trading firms.
