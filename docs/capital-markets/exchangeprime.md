# exchangePRIME

<div className="doc-image-frame">
  <img src="/img/project-grid/exchange-thumb.jpg" alt="exchangePRIME" />
</div>

exchangePRIME is a highly-optimized and ultra-low latency hybrid securities
exchange platform. It can act as Exchange, ECN, MTF (Europe), or ATS
(Canada). It is designed to be operated by any investment firm or market
operator — brokers, market makers, investment banks, stock exchanges — that
brings together multiple third-party buying and selling interests in
financial instruments.

The platform is built on a rule-based, distributed, and fault tolerant
technology, enabling it to be highly reliable, scalable, and flexible. It
also provides sub-microsecond connectivity for HFT and algorithmic trading
firms.

## The business case

- **Enter the venue business without a decade of R&D.** Whether you are a
  broker launching an internal crossing network, a market maker starting an
  ATS, or a firm seeking an MTF licence in Europe, the platform maps to your
  regulatory model out of the box.
- **Latency is the product.** Sub-microsecond connectivity means the
  algorithmic and HFT clients — the highest-volume participants — have a
  reason to connect, and to stay.
- **Fault tolerance is a commercial promise.** A venue that goes down for
  even minutes loses both reputation and flow. The distributed, fault
  tolerant core is what makes your uptime guarantee credible.
- **Rules without re-engineering.** The rule-based core lets you list new
  instruments and adjust market models as regulations evolve — configuration
  and rules, not code forks.
