# Sentin8

<div className="doc-image-frame">
  <img src="/img/project-grid/sentin8.jpg" alt="Sentin8" />
</div>

Sentin8 is the post-quantum, zero-static-secret platform for regulated
multi-vertical software. One identity plane, live authorization, and mandatory
tenant isolation.

## What it is

Sentin8 rethinks the assumptions most platforms are built on. Instead of
static API keys, long-lived database passwords, and bolt-on tenancy filters,
Sentin8 makes **zero static secrets** and **mandatory tenant isolation** the
non-negotiable substrate of every service that runs on it.

## The business case

- **Reduce breach exposure.** Static secrets are involved in the majority of
  supply-chain and lateral-movement breaches. Eliminating them wholesale —
  not just rotating them — removes the attack surface permanently.
- **Accelerate audits.** Every authorization decision is recorded with its
  evidence; compliance reporting becomes a query, not a project.
- **Expand into regulated markets faster.** Because tenancy isolation is
  mandatory (not configurable), you can enter finance, healthcare, and
  government markets without re-architecting.
- **One platform, many products.** Verticals like Bank8, Exchange8, and
  Hotel8 inherit the identity plane — meaning one login, one policy engine,
  and one audit store across your whole portfolio.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Post-quantum cryptography** | Crypto-agility and PQ-safe key exchange ahead of regulatory deadlines. |
| **Zero static secrets** | Nothing to leak, rotate, or forget — secrets live only in live exchange. |
| **One identity plane** | A single source of truth for subjects, roles, and policy across verticals. |
| **Live authorization** | Policy changes and revocations apply immediately everywhere. |
| **Mandatory tenant isolation** | Every workload is isolated by default; multi-tenancy cannot be misconfigured away. |
| **Complete audit trail** | Evidence-grade audit journaling on every action. |

## Who it's for

- **Regulated fintechs and banks** that need provable isolation and
  post-quantum readiness on a deadline.
- **Platform companies** operating multiple products that want one security
  model instead of five.
- **Government and healthcare operators** where a tenancy leak is an
  existential event.
