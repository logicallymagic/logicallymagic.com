# Open Banking AI Data Access Platform

<div className="doc-image-frame">
  <img src="/img/project-grid/OpenBanking.jpg" alt="Open Banking" />
</div>

The Open Banking AI Data Access Platform provides standards-compliant,
consent-driven access to financial data — fully FAPI 2.0 compliant and
usable by both fintechs and enterprises around the world.

## The business case

- **Compliance is the moat, not the obstacle.** FAPI 2.0 is the security
  profile regulators (and the big banks) trust. Meeting it out of the box
  removes the 12–18 month certification detour that kills most open
  banking roadmaps.
- **Consent-driven data access opens partner revenue.** Once customers can
  grant — and revoke — access to their data safely, aggregators, lenders,
  and PFM products can build on your rails.
- **AI turns raw feeds into decisions.** The platform doesn't stop at
  plumbing: AI layers turn transaction and account data into affordability
  scores, cash-flow analytics, and product recommendations — the products
  fintechs actually monetize.
- **Works for both sides.** Fintechs consume the data; enterprises (banks,
  insurers) expose it. One platform serves both roles.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **FAPI 2.0 compliance** | The security profile banks and regulators accept. |
| **Consent management** | Customer-granted, revocable, auditable data access. |
| **AI analytics layer** | Cash-flow insight, affordability, and recommendations from raw data. |
| **Enterprise & fintech modes** | Deploy as a data holder, a data consumer, or both. |
