# Bill8

Bill8 is the billing and payments vertical of the Sentin8 platform:
merchants, checkout, recurring billing, a double-entry money engine, signed
webhooks, and fraud screening.

## What it is

A complete monetization stack for platform businesses — everything between
"customer decides to buy" and "money is in your ledger," built on the same
double-entry discipline as Bank8.

## The business case

- **Recurring revenue without a billing team.** Recurring billing, proration,
  dunning, and plan changes are solved problems here — your team focuses on
  growth, not invoice edge cases.
- **A ledger that reconciles itself.** The double-entry money engine means
  your finance team's numbers match your product's numbers, always.
- **Fraud screening before the chargeback.** Screening runs at checkout, so
  fraudulent transactions are declined before they cost you fees and
  disputes.
- **Webhooks your engineers can trust.** Signed, ordered webhook delivery
  removes the "did you get my event?" support tickets that plague payment
  integrations.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Merchants & checkout** | Hosted and embedded checkout with multiple merchant support. |
| **Recurring billing** | Subscriptions, proration, trials, and dunning out of the box. |
| **Double-entry money engine** | Reconciliation-grade accounting for every charge and refund. |
| **Signed webhooks** | Cryptographically verifiable integration events. |
| **Fraud screening** | Risk checks at checkout to cut chargebacks and abuse. |

## Who it's for

- **SaaS and platform companies** that need enterprise-grade billing without
  building it in-house.
- **Marketplaces** managing multiple merchants with split money flows.
- **Fintechs embedding payments** into vertical products.
