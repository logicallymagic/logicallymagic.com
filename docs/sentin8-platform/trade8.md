# Trade8

Trade8 is the professional trading vertical of the Sentin8 platform: order
management, broker connectivity, synchronous pre-trade risk, market data,
and day P&L.

## What it is

A complete order and execution management stack for professional desks —
the workflow layer that sits between your traders and the venues, with risk
checks that happen *before* the order leaves the building.

## The business case

- **Synchronous pre-trade risk is non-negotiable.** Fat-finger and
  runaway-algorithm losses are preventable; Trade8 enforces limits before an
  order can hit the wire, not after it clears.
- **One workspace for every market.** Order management, market data, and
  day P&L in one vertical means traders stop stitching spreadsheets
  together at 4pm.
- **Broker connectivity that compounds.** Each connected broker becomes
  available to every desk on the platform, turning integration work into a
  shared asset.
- **Real-time P&L.** Day P&L updates with the market, giving risk managers
  and traders the same live picture.

## Core capabilities

| Capability | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Order management** | Full OMS workflow — staging, routing, amendment, and cancels. |
| **Broker connectivity** | Standardized connectivity across brokers with one integration model. |
| **Synchronous pre-trade risk** | Limits enforced in the hot path — violations never reach the venue. |
| **Market data** | Consolidated market data feeding both traders and risk. |
| **Day P&L** | Live, per-desk, per-trader P&L for management and compliance. |

## Who it's for

- **Prop trading firms and family offices** that need institutional risk
  controls without institutional procurement.
- **Hedge funds** unifying execution across brokers.
- **Fintechs** offering trading infrastructure to professional clients.
