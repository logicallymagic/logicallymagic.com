• sorPRIME

    Machine Learning Smart Order Router

sorPRIME (SOR') is a smart order router that finds the best way to execute a trade using predefined business rules and AI (machine learning) to enhance the investors’ ability to access liquidity in the market and receive the best prices and execution. It is a flexible, highly extensible, and ultra-low latency smart order routing platform designed to support the varied trading needs of execution venues, sell-side and buy-side firms.

SOR' focuses on:

  • Best Execution – Use of machine learning to optimize the numerical details per stock.
  • Handling of Dark Pools – Use of machine learning to predict distribution of liquidity in the pool.
  • Risk management.
