# iReal.Ai

<div className="doc-image-frame">
  <img src="/img/project-grid/ireal-thumb.jpg" alt="iReal.Ai" />

iReal.Ai is an interactive and fast predictive real estate AI platform —
real-time valuations and market insight built for the people who actually
price property: realtors, brokerages, and lenders.

## The business case

- **Speed wins listings.** An AVM that answers in seconds — not overnight
  batch jobs — changes the listing conversation: walk into a pitch with a
  live, interactive model on the kitchen table.
- **Predictive, not just comparable.** Classical AVMs match comparables;
  iReal.Ai predicts where the market is going, which is the question every
  seller and every lender is actually asking.
- **Interactive builds trust.** Adjust the inputs with the client watching —
  renovations, market shifts, school districts — and the valuation updates
  live. Explainability is a sales feature, not a compliance chore.
- **Platform-grade delivery.** Built with the same engineering DNA as our
  capital markets systems: high-throughput data pipelines, fast models, and
  an interface designed for screen real estate.

## Who it's for

- **Realtors and brokerages** that want a defensible, interactive pricing
  tool in every listing presentation.
- **Lenders** needing fast, consistent, collateral-grade valuations.
- **iReal** — our real estate venture partner — deploys it in production
  for the Toronto market.
