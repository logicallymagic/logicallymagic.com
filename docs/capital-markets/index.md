## Why the PRIME suite

- **Proven in the hardest domain.** Capital markets software has the
  strictest latency, correctness, and regulatory requirements of any
  industry. Building here made us better everywhere else.
- **A complete execution stack.** Operate a venue (exchangePRIME), run a
  brokerage (brokerPRIME), give traders a workspace (traderPRIME), and
  optimize execution (sorPRIME) — the products interlock but stand alone.
- **AI where it pays.** Machine learning is applied to the problems where it
  measurably improves outcomes: best execution, liquidity prediction, and
  risk.
