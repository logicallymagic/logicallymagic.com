Health8 is the eighth vertical of the Sentin8 platform, currently in design.

## What it is

The healthcare vertical of the Sentin8 platform — bringing the same
zero-static-secret security, mandatory tenant isolation, and live
authorization that power Bank8 and Exchange8 to healthcare data and
workflows.

## The business case

- **Healthcare's bar is the highest.** Patient data demands isolation and
  auditability that most platforms approximate. Sentin8's mandatory tenant
  isolation makes those properties architectural facts, not configuration.
- **Design status is your opportunity.** The vertical is currently in
  design — early partners can shape scope, integrations, and the rollout
  roadmap directly with the team.
- **Consistency across your stack.** Organizations already running other
  Sentin8 verticals will get healthcare workflows with the same identity
  plane, policy engine, and audit store — one security review, many
  products.
